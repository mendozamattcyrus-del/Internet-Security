01

Use Strong, Unique Passwords

Create passwords with 12+ characters mixing letters, numbers, and symbols. Never reuse passwords across services.

02

Enable Email Two-Factor Authentication

Add a second layer of security with email verification codes on every account that supports it.

03

Keep Software Updated

Enable automatic updates for your OS, browsers, and apps. Patches often fix critical security vulnerabilities.

04

Beware of Suspicious Links

Hover over links before clicking. Verify sender addresses. Never click unexpected links in emails or messages.

05

Scan Files Before Opening

Always scan downloaded files, especially executables (.exe, .bat, .vbs). Check file extensions carefully for double extensions.

06

Run Regular Device Scans

Use the Device Virus Scanner weekly to detect malware, suspicious scripts, and privacy threats on your system.

07

Verify Email Senders

Check the actual email address, not just the display name. Phishers often spoof familiar names.

08

Review App Permissions

Regularly audit which apps have access to your accounts, files, and personal data. Revoke what you don't need.
